OBSERVATIONS

1. Sentence embeddings convert text into numerical vectors that represent their semantic meaning.

2. The 20 sentences were converted into 384-dimensional embedding vectors using the Sentence Transformer model.

3. Cosine similarity was used to compare the meaning of different sentence pairs.

4. Space-related sentences showed high similarity because they discuss space exploration, planets and scientific technologies.

5. Environment-related sentences showed high similarity because they discuss climate change, carbon emissions and renewable energy.

6. Health-related sentences showed good similarity because regular exercise and balanced diet are related to a healthy lifestyle.

7. Sports-related sentences such as football and cricket showed similarity because both belong to the sports domain.

8. Sentences from unrelated topics generally produced lower similarity scores.

9. The results show that embeddings can identify semantic relationships even when sentences do not contain exactly the same words.

10. Therefore, text embeddings are useful for semantic search, recommendation systems and LLM applications.
